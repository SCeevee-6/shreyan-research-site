My mathematical interests developed from competitive mathematics into a deeper interest in
abstraction, proof, and mathematical structure. I became increasingly interested in questions that
cannot be resolved by computation alone, particularly problems involving algebraic combinatorics,
group actions, and association schemes.

My research extends into computational biology, where I investigate the reliability of
machine-learning predictions and the mathematical conditions under which scientific models can be
trusted. I am particularly interested in the gap between predictive accuracy and scientific
validity.

I also write poetry and prose and study cello. My notebooks are intended to document the process of
thinking itself: proofs, computational experiments, research developments, and creative work.

## Interests

In mathematics: algebraic combinatorics, association schemes, generalized polygons, group actions
and orbital configurations, discrete mathematics, proof, and exact symbolic computation. In science:
computational biology, protein language models, the reliability of machine learning, scientific
computing, differential equations, and the mathematical verification of scientific models.
