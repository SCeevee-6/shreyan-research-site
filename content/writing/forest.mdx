title: Forest
kind: poem
order: 3
---

for years, the forest thrived
growing
lively
green

the rain poured down
making the forest
happy

the sun shone bright
making the forest
happy

the animals played
making the forest
happy

but one year
the animals turned savage
clawing at the forest
angry

but the rain poured
and the sun shone
making the forest
happy

but the next year
the sun turned harsh
immense heat game down
goading the animals
but the rain still poured
keeping the forest
happy

the rain kept pouring
and pouring
trying to stop the sun and animals
but the forest felt bad for the rain
and told it to stop

and despite the rain's protests
the rain gave up one day
and the forest set to flames
dying
gray
making the forest
happy
