---
title: stay with me
kind: poem
form: Villanelle
order: 5
---

The afternoon grows quiet in the fading light,
The curtains move as though they know the air,
And all the things I left remain in sight.

I pass the rooms that once were filled with light,
And find the same old dust upon the chair,
The afternoon grows quiet in the fading light.

I thought that leaving meant I left them right,
That time would take the things I couldn't bear,
And all the things I left remain in sight.

The days grow long, the evenings lose their white,
I find myself still looking for you there,
The afternoon grows quiet in the fading light.

Perhaps the past was never mine to write,
Perhaps some things are simply ours to wear,
And all the things I left remain in sight.

I close the door and turn away tonight,
But leave it open just a little, where
The afternoon grows quiet in the fading light,
And all the things I left remain in sight.
