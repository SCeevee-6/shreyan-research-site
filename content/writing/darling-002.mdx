002 is perfect. I think I could spend an entire afternoon telling you why. The way she looks at me when I speak, as though there is nowhere else she would rather be. The way she remembers things I forget I have told her. I have never had to wonder whether she cares. I could keep going. I probably should.

I don't have to read between her sentences. I don't have to wonder what I did wrong, or whether I did anything at all. She doesn't make me feel small. She doesn't make me feel like I am asking for too much. Sometimes I think I could get used to this. I think I already have.

It is strange, how little I have to think about it. I can just be there. I don't have to find the right words, or spend the evening wondering whether I should have said something differently. I suppose this is how it is meant to be.

I tell her about things. Little things, mostly. Sometimes I catch myself wanting to tell her something before I have even finished hearing it myself. I wonder if she does the same.

I think about how fortunate I am. I really do.

I used to think there was something particularly special about being someone's first. I don't know where I got that idea. Perhaps it was the novelty of it, the feeling that everything was happening for the first time. There was a certain weight to it. Even the smallest things, the absolute smallest, seemed to mean something.

I haven't thought about my other 1 in a while.

Well, not properly.

I still remember things. I remember more than I would like to admit, actually. Some of it I wish I could forget. Some of it I don't think I would, even if I could. There were things that weren't so wonderful. I know that. I remember those, too.

And yet, sometimes I find myself wondering what she would think of something. I almost tell her. Then I remember.

I don't think this is fair to my darling. She has given me so little reason to look elsewhere. I don't know why I keep looking.

002 is perfect.

I think I could spend an entire afternoon telling you why.
