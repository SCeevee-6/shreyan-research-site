---
title: The Vase
kind: poem
order: 2
---

late one night
I opened the door
to bring in an ornate crystal vase
intricate with flowery etching
beautiful in every regard

The vase brought joy
into my mundane life
I turned on my lights
to care to the vase
hours in a day
perfect love

I let people into my abode
once more
lights focused on the vase
the flowery etching
gleaming attractively

but why
why should others look at my vase
my joy and love
as they may take it
as their own

I closed the door
and turned off the lights
protecting my precious, perfect vase
unaware
my vase was gone
once the lights were turned off

yet I still yearn
for my crystal vase
my perfect vase
in the dark once more
the door forever closed
