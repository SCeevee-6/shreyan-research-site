These are exact computations, not a written classification. The 1755-point action is [Note 2](/mathematics/tits-1755); this
note covers the other actions of $T={}^2F_4(2)'$ on cosets of its maximal subgroups.

## Commutative or not

For a transitive group the orbitals already form a coherent configuration. On cosets of a maximal
subgroup $M$ it is commutative iff the permutation character $1_M^T$ is multiplicity-free; its rank
is $\langle\pi,\pi\rangle=\sum m_\chi^2$, and the number of self-paired orbitals is
$\sum \nu(\chi)\, m_\chi$, where $\nu$ is the Frobenius–Schur indicator. In every case the rank
computed from double cosets equals $\langle\pi,\pi\rangle$, and the self-paired count equals the
indicator sum.

| $M$ (max) | degree | rank | self-paired | configuration |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $L_3(3){:}2$ (1, 2) | 1600 | 4 | 4 | commutative, symmetric |
| $2.[2^8].5.4$ (3) | 1755 | 5 | 5 | commutative, symmetric |
| $L_2(25)$ (4) | 2304 | 7 | 5 | commutative |
| $2^2.[2^8].S_3$ (5) | 2925 | 6 | 6 | commutative, symmetric |
| $A_6.2^2$ (6, 7) | 12480 | 24 | 16 | non-commutative |
| $5^2{:}4A_4$ (8) | 14976 | 25 | 17 | non-commutative |

The $L_2(25)$ action on $2304$ points is commutative but not symmetric: it has one pair of
non-self-paired orbitals, which matches the complex-conjugate pair of characters $27, 27'$. Only the
1755-point action is metric.

## Fusions of the commutative actions

Every partition of the non-trivial orbitals was tested for closure with exact integers.

- **Degree 1600** (both classes of $L_3(3){:}2$). Three proper partitions; one closes,
  $\{1,3\}\,|\,\{2\}$. It gives a strongly regular graph with parameters $(1600,351,94,72)$. This is
  known: Crnković–Rukavina–Švob list it with full automorphism group $T$.
- **Degree 2304** ($L_2(25)$). Of $201$ proper partitions, three close: the orbital scheme of $T.2$
  (rank $6$, non-symmetric), its symmetrisation (rank $6$), and the symmetrisation of the $T.2$
  scheme (rank $5$). None is $P$- or $Q$-polynomial, and there is no rank-3 fusion.
- **Degree 2925** ($2^2.[2^8].S_3$, the line stabiliser of $\mathrm{GO}(2,4)$). Of $50$ proper
  partitions, one closes, $\{1\}\,|\,\{2\}\,|\,\{3\}\,|\,\{4,5\}$: the distance scheme of the dual
  octagon $\mathrm{GO}(4,2)$, with array $\{12,8,8,8;1,1,1,3\}$. It is $P$-polynomial in the natural
  order but not $Q$-polynomial, as De Bruyn–Vanhove predicts for thick regular near octagons.
- **Degree 1755.** No proper fusion, by Corollary C of [Note 1](/mathematics/fusion-theorem).

## The non-commutative algebras

For a transitive group the orbital algebra is the centraliser algebra $\operatorname{End}_T(1_M^T)$,
which is semisimple over $\mathbb{C}$ and is a direct sum of matrix algebras $M_m(\mathbb{C})$ over
the constituents of $1_M^T$, with $m$ the multiplicity. The centre was computed exactly from the
integer structure constants of the orbital algebra, independently of the character table:

<Proposition n={1}>
The orbital algebra of $T$ on $12480$ points (both classes of $A_6.2^2$) has rank $24$ and centre of
dimension $13$, and is isomorphic to $11\,\mathbb{C}\oplus M_2(\mathbb{C})\oplus M_3(\mathbb{C})$.
The orbital algebra on $14976$ points ($5^2{:}4A_4$) has rank $25$ and centre of dimension $13$, and
is isomorphic to $9\,\mathbb{C}\oplus 4\,M_2(\mathbb{C})$.
</Proposition>

With rank $r$ and centre dimension $z$, the block sizes $m_1,\dots,m_z$ satisfy $\sum m_i^2=r$; for
$(24,13)$ and $(25,13)$ the only solutions are the ones above, and they agree with the multiplicities
from the library character table ($351$ three times and $675$ twice on $12480$ points; $351$, $675$,
$1300$, $1300'$ twice each on $14976$ points).

## Fusions of the non-commutative configurations

For every transposition-closed union $S$ of non-trivial orbitals, the coarsest fusion in which $S$ is
a union of classes was computed by refinement inside the orbital algebra.

- On $12480$ points (both classes), every $S$ gives back the full rank-24 configuration: there is no
  fusion strictly between the orbital configuration and the trivial rank-2 scheme.
- On $14976$ points, every $S$ gives either the full rank-25 configuration or one rank-16 fusion,
  which is not commutative. So every fusion of rank at least $3$ refines that rank-16 configuration —
  the orbital configuration of ${}^2F_4(2)$ — and is non-commutative.

In particular neither action has a commutative fusion other than the trivial one, consistent with
the empty search for strongly regular graphs in [Note 4](/mathematics/results-ledger).
