# Product

Personal research site of Shreyan Chethan, a high-school student in Allen, Texas: research in
algebraic combinatorics, computational biology and verified equation discovery; mathematics notes;
poems and prose; cello. Readers: professors, competition and fair judges, peers.

Mode: Read for notes and research pages; Experience for the cover and the appendix toys.
Constraints: static export on Vercel; no analytics, no cookies, no third-party requests; every fact,
number, poem and date comes from the author or the deposited records — nothing invented.
